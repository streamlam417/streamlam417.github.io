Clipper privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What Clipper is

Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload and manage short videos on the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Clipper receives permission to upload videos to, and read basic statistics (views, subscribers, video status) of, the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It uses this only to upload the owner's videos, schedule them, and report how they perform to the owner.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with or sent to third parties, other than Google itself when Clipper calls the YouTube API.

Other people

Clipper does not let anyone else sign in and does not collect personal data from viewers or other users.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
